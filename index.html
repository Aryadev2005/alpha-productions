Questions people ask

Do you shoot in the studio or at our place?

Both. Newborn and portrait shoots work well in the studio, and we travel to homes, halls, churches, temples and venues for events.

Is it safe to photograph a newborn?

Yes. Newborn sessions go at the baby's pace, with breaks for feeding and settling. Parents stay close the whole time.

Can we customise a package?

Yes. Every package can be adjusted to your functions, venue and number of days. Tell us what you need and we'll quote it.

Do you cover corporate events?

Yes. We cover conferences, launches and team events, and film brand and industrial videos and podcasts.
